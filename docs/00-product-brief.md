# Product Brief · AIRTRUTH

## Vision

Turn unilateral "Bad Weather" flight delay claims into verifiable, cryptographically signed passenger rights packets backed by public ADS-B radar, METAR weather reports, parallel runway takeoff logs, and tail-number rotations.

## Target Users

- **Air Passengers & Travel Disruption Claimants** checking whether an SMS weather excuse is supported by radar evidence.
- **Flight Rights Platforms & Consumer Advocacy Apps** needing automated EU261, UK261, US DOT Part 260, and DGCA CAR rebuttal evidence bundles.
- **Airlines, OTAs & TMC Portals** embedding honest disruption explanations and proactive rebooking/statutory credit flows via `@airtruth/js`.

## Key Workflows

1. Select disrupted carrier sandbox (**IndiGo**, **Delta**, **British Airways**).
2. Scrub the live flight timeline from prior hub rotation through gate pushback.
3. Trigger **"Airline claims weather"** to flash live radar contradictions (`8` parallel runway departures, `VT-IFU` crew FDTL timeout in Mumbai `6h` prior).
4. Inspect and download the SHA-256 signed statutory payout claim packet containing Mode-S ADS-B coordinates.
